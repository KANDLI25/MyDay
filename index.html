MyDay Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

MyDay is a personal task, lists, reminders, and finance tracking application. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

Information you enter may include your name, age, country, tasks, lists, reminders, debts, lending records, payments, and financial balance. This information is stored locally on your device. The app is designed to encrypt its local app data.

No account or online collection

MyDay does not require an online account. The app does not send your entered information to a MyDay server. The app does not include advertising or analytics services.

App lock and biometrics

Your app lock credential is used to protect access to the app. If you enable biometric unlock, Android handles the biometric verification. MyDay does not receive or store your fingerprint or face data.

Reminders and notifications

If you allow notifications, MyDay can show reminders you create. Notification content may be visible on your lock screen depending on your device settings. You can manage notification permissions in Android settings.

Currency conversion

If you change your currency, conversion is based on the rate you enter in the app. MyDay does not fetch exchange rates from an online service.

Backups

If you create an encrypted backup, the backup file is saved to the location you choose. Keep the file and its password private. Anyone who obtains the file may attempt to guess its password offline. MyDay cannot recover a forgotten backup password.

Retention and deletion

Your information remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app data. Items moved to the app's trash may remain there for up to 30 days before permanent deletion. Exported backup files are not deleted when you remove information from the app; manage those files separately in the location where you saved them.

Children

MyDay is a general-purpose personal organization and finance tracking app. It is not designed to collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data handling change. The latest version will be published at this page.
